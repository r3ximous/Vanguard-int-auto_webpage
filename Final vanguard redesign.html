Who we are

About Vanguard

We specialise in vehicle maintenance, diagnostics, and mechanical repairs for German, British, Japanese, and Korean vehicles. Our experience covers everything from routine servicing and air conditioning repairs to complex engine and transmission replacements.

Through our partnerships with trusted specialist workshops, we ensure that every vehicle receives the appropriate expertise and attention it deserves.

Transparency, trust, and customer satisfaction are at the heart of our service. We believe in keeping our customers informed at every stage, providing clear explanations, regular updates, and photographic evidence of our findings and completed work. Our goal is not only to maintain and repair your vehicle, but also to help you understand its needs, make informed decisions, and feel confident in the service you receive.

Your vehicle is in capable hands, with honest advice, clear communication, and quality service from start to finish.

What we do

Good work begins with good listening.

MR

Mechanical Repairs & Maintenance

From routine oil changes and regular servicing to complete engine and transmission replacements, we provide reliable maintenance and repair solutions to keep your vehicle running smoothly.

ECU

Computer Diagnostics (ECU)

Identify potential issues with your vehicle through computerised diagnostic scans. A diagnostic scan and clear explanation of fault codes are included free of charge with any repair work or service carried out by us, helping you understand the problem before proceeding.

AC

Air Conditioning Services

From air conditioning gas top-ups to fault diagnosis and complete system repairs, we help keep your vehicle's air conditioning performing efficiently and your drive comfortable.

PB

Auto Painting & Bodywork

Restore your vehicle's appearance with professional repainting, dent repairs, and panel repairs or replacements. Whether you're dealing with minor cosmetic damage or more extensive bodywork, we help get your vehicle looking its best.
